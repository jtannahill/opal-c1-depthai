Calibration

Moved the camera? Recalibrate re-sweeps focus, re-detects the water automatically (or uses boxes you draw), clears the old location's bearing fit, and restarts detection. Auto-detect water alone just refreshes the water outline.

Picture

Applies live to the river view. Auto exposure blows out a bright window seen from a dark room, so these are manual.

Camera position

Paste GPS from Google Maps (right-click the spot, click the coordinates) and the direction the camera faces. Bearing ticks turn on immediately; sightings then refine heading and lens.
Nudge heading
Best: click Align next to a ship in the AIS list, then click that ship in the image. Two ships far apart also fit the lens.
