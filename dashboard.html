Live view

4K H.264 straight off the camera, about 17 fps while detection runs (30+ with it paused). No overlays on this view, and a few seconds behind live.
Blue outline: water watched by the detector. Green: moving boats (sightings). Gray: detected but not moving yet. Green line: boat's path over the last 90 s. Orange labels: where AIS says each ship is (needs camera position; stopped ships in gray). Centre reticle shows where the camera points and how far that is; range lines mark 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 km on the water. Drag a rectangle to zoom to it, click to zoom to a point, double-click to reset. Native 4K pixels, no upscaling on the server.

Calibration

Moved the camera? Recalibrate re-sweeps focus, re-detects the water automatically (or uses boxes you draw), clears the old location's bearing fit, and restarts detection. Auto-detect water alone just refreshes the water outline.

Lookup

Click Measure, then click anywhere in the view for its compass bearing, and its coordinates once the ground plane is fitted. Or paste a position or bearing below to mark it in the view.

Map

Top-down view from the camera. Give your height above the water for an immediate estimate, or fit it properly from sightings whose range AIS pins down. Estimated distances are shown with a ~.

Picture
